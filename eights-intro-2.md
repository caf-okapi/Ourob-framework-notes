[... What is winning?](eights-intro.md) 


In the Ourob Framework, that would be survivability and normal-flow. Personal security and happiness. In other words, you could say it's all about two things — making sure oneself and those one cares about are safe and in circumstances where they're able to reliably survive (and not suffer bad health, etc.) and to be happy and in a good quality of life. 

Those are basically the definitions we'll be using for the Ourob Framework (though we'll go more into that later).

As mentioned before, this is a Framework of personality, behavior, and actions, aimed at achieving winning. And so you could say achieving this personal security and happiness are the bases of this entire Framework. It is built from the ground-up to rationally (and in practical steps) achieve these two realities, taking them as the priorities of life and the foundation for everything in the Framework.

So, for those who agree that these two bases are the priorities of life, this Framework may be somewhat useful. And for those who don't, maybe not so much ...
